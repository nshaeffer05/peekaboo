Common questions

How do I add a friend? Profile → Friends. Show your code for them to scan, scan theirs, or search their username. They have to accept before either of you can send anything.

How long does a photo last? Eleven seconds from when it is opened, once. Unopened photos vanish after 24 hours.

Can I save a photo? No. There is no gallery, no saving, and screenshots are blocked while a photo is on screen.

Reporting a photo or blocking someone. Tap the flag while a photo is open to report it. To block, open Friends, tap the person and choose Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Deleting your account. Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and permanent.
