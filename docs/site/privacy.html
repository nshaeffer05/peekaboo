Peekaboo Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

What we collect

  • Email address and password – to sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider.
  • Username – shown to people who search for you or scan your code.
  • Photos you send – held only until every recipient has opened them, or for 24 hours if they never do, then deleted. We keep no copy. A reported photo is kept until we have reviewed it.
  • Friendships, groups and blocks – needed to decide who can send to whom.
  • A push notification token – so we can tell you a photo arrived. Only if you allow notifications.
  • Reports you file – to moderate content.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos from your library, or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The app never saves photos to your device and blocks screenshots while a photo is on screen.

Who sees what

Anyone in the app can find your username. Only friends you have accepted can send you photos, and you can only send to them. A photo is shown once, to the people you chose, for eleven seconds. The sender cannot view it again after sending.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database, file storage and authentication) in the United States. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple. Emails about your account are sent through Resend.

How long

Photos: until opened, or 24 hours at most. A "sent / opened" line without the image is kept for a day. Everything else: for as long as you have an account. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) removes your profile, friendships, groups, unopened photos and push token immediately.

Children

Peekaboo is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Email us to get a copy of your data or to have it deleted; in-app deletion is faster.
