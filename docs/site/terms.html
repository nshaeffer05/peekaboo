You own what you take. By sending a photo you let us deliver it, once, to the friends you chose. It is deleted after they open it, or after 24 hours. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Do not send anything that is sexual, violent, hateful, harassing, illegal, or that targets a real person. Any recipient can report a photo or block a sender, reported photos are held for review, and we review every report within 24 hours. We close accounts that break these rules.
